At Thelix I build the internal platforms the group runs on. The two most recent are AI systems that went from plan to production.

Thelix Atlas

A company knowledge assistant that cites its sources

Staff ask questions in plain language. Atlas answers from the company's own documents and live Command Hub data, and every answer links back to the source.

  • Access-aware by design: per-document permissions are checked again whenever a source is opened
  • Knowledge governance: versioning, verification status, owners, archiving and reindexing
  • Retrieval pipeline: PDF, DOCX and XLSX ingestion, chunking and embeddings in PostgreSQL with pgvector
  • Streaming answers over Server-Sent Events, with status, token and citation events
  • Atlas API: a read-only API for questions about people, projects, SBUs and reviews
  • Works with both OpenAI and Anthropic models; background jobs run on BullMQ

Thelix Prospect

An AI prospecting and appointment-setting system

It finds companies that fit a brand's ideal customer profile, researches them with sources, reaches the right decision-maker by email and AI voice call, handles replies, and books qualified meetings for sales.

  • Workflow coordinator: a single shared lead record moved through each stage by queued workers
  • AI voice calling through Retell, with call records and review
  • Human in the loop: review queue, reply rules, suppression list and a global outreach stop
  • Four roles (admin, campaign owner, sales, reviewer) with scoped views, on Thelix SSO
  • ICP builder, experiments, knowledge base, meetings and an audit trail
  • Separate web and worker services on DigitalOcean App Platform
